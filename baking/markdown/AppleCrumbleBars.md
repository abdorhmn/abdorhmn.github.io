# Apple Crumble Bar Recipe

## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 Quite Large apples (or equivalent)
- [ ] Salted Butter
- [ ] Sugar
- [ ] Plain Flour
- [ ] Water

## The process

### The apple part
- Peel the apples
- Chop into small-ish chunks
- Put all apple into medium-size saucepan
- Pour just enough water to cover base of pan
- Set over medium high heat until apple stews (7-10min)
- When ready apple should be able to hold its shape but if mashed it will break down

### The crumble part    
- Soften 125g of butter ~~in the microwave~~ the professional way
- Add 50g of sugar
- Add 250g of plain flour
- Using your hands in a clamping motion mix the stuff until it's combined (bread crumb texture)

### Put them together
- Into a lined baking tray pour half the crumble
- Using your fingers press down and spread the crumble across the whole base of tray firmly
- Place apples over top
- Sprinkle the rest of the crumble

### Cooking time
- 180°C or 360°F oven for 30min
- Should just slide out when ready
- [ ] Cut up and eat the yummy crumble bars
