About

I'm an undergraduate at the University of Michigan studying electrical engineering. My interests are in embedded systems and RF and wireless communications, and I love learning and solving problems creatively through engineering. I also have a résumé :)

Right now, I build the radio and communications system for Michigan Mars Rover and work as a Student Manager and Tech Help Consultant with Information and Technology Services. Before college, I led, built, and designed electrical and mechanical systems with FRC Team 469 Las Guerrillas and contributed to the non-profit Tamkeen's website using .NET and SQL Server.

In my free time, I like exploring nature, competing in game jams, and running the educational YouTube channel Learning Comically. I'm also president of our Blacksmithing Club. I like herbal teas, fun facts, and snowy days.
